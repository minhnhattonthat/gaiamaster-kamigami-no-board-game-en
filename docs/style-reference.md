# Style reference - GaiaMaster: Kamigami no Board Game

Tone: a Capcom party board game (2000) in a light comic fantasy world. Heroes of legend and myth are summoned to
"GaiaMaster", a board game the gods run, and fight over land and money. Belial the pixie hosts and teases everyone.
The English should be snappy, funny and in character; menus and rules text plain and clear.

## Voices
| Character | Who | Voice in English | JP markers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Belial | pixie host, guide, char-select narrator, card intros | sassy, cute, catty; talks to the player as "you" | 〜わ, 〜よ, 〜かしら |
| Jiggar | young treasure hunter, street kid in love with Erina (blind daughter of the richest man; he seeks the Elixir to cure her) | brash, cheeky teen slang | おれ, 〜ぜ |
| Sinbad | bandit, womaniser, dreams of a big score | cocky, smooth, wisecracking | オレ/俺, 〜さ |
| Hayate | ninja master, killing machine | terse, cold, few words | ム…, 忍… |
| Agathe | lonely witch, clingy and creepy | soft, wistful, possessive; trailing "..." | わたし, 〜の…, 許さない |
| Meg-meg | muscle-bound warrior girl, falls in love too easily | bubbly, loud, boy-crazy; @ hearts | あたし, やっほー, 〜な！ |
| Goliath | greedy, violent, drunk, lecherous priest | blustering old-man pomp, mock-pious | 〜じゃ, わし |
| Tiara | princess, sweet on the outside, sadist inside; alter ego Angel Tiara | prim and polite, with a nasty edge; Angel Tiara is magical-girl showy | 〜ですわ, よろしくって？ |
| Galahad | paladin, honest to a fault | earnest, formal, noble | 騎士として, 〜う！ |
| Son Gokuh | the Monkey King | swaggering, refers to himself as "this great one" / "the great Son" | 俺さま, 孫さま |
| Yasutsuna | samurai from the Far East | stiff, formal, old-fashioned (no thee/thou) | 〜でござる, 殿 |
| Dante | Demon Lord, bored and grandiose | lofty, theatrical, archaic touch allowed ("Fool...") | 我, そなた, 〜よかろう |
