# Synopsis - GaiaMaster: Kamigami no Board Game (Capcom, PS1, 1998)

A Monopoly-style board game. The gods hold "GaiaMaster", a contest that pulls heroes from legends and other worlds
onto living game boards; players buy land, build facilities (hospital, knights, monastery, alchemy, smithy, bank),
charge tolls, play weapon/skill cards in battles and event/symbol cards on the board, and win by assets or by
bankrupting the rest. Belial, a sharp-tongued pixie, hosts: she introduces each scenario, comments on players and
narrates the character select.

## Cast (playable; official English names from the staff roll)
- **Jiggar** the treasure hunter - street kid who loves a princess far above his station; wants a town without kings.
- **Sinbad** the bandit - womaniser chasing a get-rich-quick dream.
- **Hayate** the ninja master - a lower-rank ninja (下忍) used and thrown away by his clan; bitter, deadly.
- **Agathe** the witch - alone since her mother died; cursed people to death a century ago; wants to be loved.
- **Meg-meg** the warrior - muscle girl, falls for every man, dreams of a happy marriage and a big dog.
- **Goliath** the rogue priest - greedy, violent, drunk, lecherous; dodges his debts.
- **Princess Tiara** - polite, two-faced, secretly sadistic; fights evil as the masked "Angel Tiara".
- **Galahad** the paladin - honest to a fault, works hard and never gets promoted; serves his people.
- **Son Gokuh** the magic monkey - the Monkey King, the Great Sage Equal to Heaven; rival of the god Jiro Shinkun.
- **Yasutsuna** the samurai - from the Far East (日の本); sent abroad by his lord to win GaiaMaster; comrade Kanemitsu.
- **Dante** the diabolus - the Demon Lord, plays out of boredom; the gods and humans unite against him.
- **Belial** the pixie - host, not playable.
